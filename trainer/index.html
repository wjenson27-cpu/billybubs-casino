Player and Banker drawing rules

Eight decks, punto banco, EZ Baccarat with no commission. Face cards and tens count 0, aces 1, other cards their number. The total is the sum modulo 10. A natural 8 or 9 ends the hand: nobody draws. Otherwise Player draws on 0–5 and stands on 6–7. If Player stands, Banker draws on 0–5 and stands on 6–7. If Player draws, Banker follows the table. Banker wins pay 1 to 1, and a three-card Banker 7 pushes the Banker bet. Tie pays 8 to 1. Dragon 7 pays 40 to 1 when Banker wins with a three-card 7. Panda 8 pays 25 to 1 when Player wins with a three-card 8. A Player or Banker bet pushes on a tie.

Payouts and what the zeros do

Straight 35:1, split 17:1, street 11:1, corner 8:1, six line 5:1, dozens and columns 2:1, red, black, odd, even, 1–18, and 19–36 pay 1:1. The American 5-number basket (0, 00, 1, 2, 3) pays 6:1 and is not on a single-zero wheel. Zero and double zero lose every outside bet. Practice chips have no cash value. The spin is a uniform draw over the pockets on the wheel you picked.
